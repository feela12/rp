Q: HOW CAN I CONTACT CUSTOMER SUPPORT?

A: You can reach our customer support team by emailing info@iczz.com

Q: WHAT IS YOUR RETURN POLICY?

A: We offer a 30-day return policy for unused and unworn items. Please contact our support team for assistance.

Q: DO YOU OFFER INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING?

A: Yes, we ship internationally. Shipping costs and delivery times may vary based on your location.

Q: HOW CAN I TRACK MY ORDER?

A: Once your order has been shipped, you will receive a tracking number via email. You can use this number to track your package on the carrier's website.

Q: WHAT PAYMENT METHODS DO YOU ACCEPT?

A: We accept major credit cards, PayPal, and Apple Pay for online purchases.

Q: CAN I CHANGE OR CANCEL MY ORDER?

A: If you need to change or cancel your order, please contact our customer support team as soon as possible. We will do our best to accommodate your request.

Q: DO YOU HAVE A PHYSICAL STORE?

A: Currently, we operate exclusively online and do not have a physical retail location.
